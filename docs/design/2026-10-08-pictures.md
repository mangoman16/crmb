# UI spec: pictures — the children's and the team's, „Dein Foto" and the switch by age; with the design audit's „Mehr" at attendance and the face beside the greeting (ui-ux-designer, 2026-10-08)

**Status.** Built on 2026-10-08 with ADR 0031, in the commit that adds this document. docs-writer placed it from three specifications of that day: the children's pictures; the additions after the owner's answers (the team's pictures, „Dein Foto", the switch worded by age); and the design audit's N3 and N7. The designers' reports are left out, and what the build changed is corrected in the text and listed in §11. The code holds the markup and the styles; this document holds no second copy of them.

**What was measured.** Prototypes on the real pages of `b4e457e` and `4b41c1b`, before the build, in Chromium 141 with MariaDB 10.11.14 and PHP 8.4.26: the new markup and styles injected, drawn placeholder faces rather than photos, and `tests/mobile.mjs`'s own rules at 320 and 390 px, light and dark (§12). **Nothing was measured on an iPhone**: not the camera, not a photo's size, not the shrinking, not VoiceOver.

**Who, where, in their words.**
- A trainer new to a group, at attendance, the phone in one hand and the children in front of her: „Wer ist Mia, wer ist Emma?"
- A child on the sofa: „Ich will mein Foto da drin haben."
- A parent: „Wer sieht das Foto von meinem Kind?"

## 1. The picture card on the child's page

**Where.** On the Profil tab, after „Noch zu ergänzen" (a family) or „Noch zu tun" (staff) and before „Persönliche Daten", as a card of its own (`#picture`), outside the student form, because its forms post on their own. At the top, because a face is who somebody is, and adding one is something a child does on the first visit. Compact, because the old card, before round 2, took a whole screen at 320.

**Layout at 320.**
1. One row, 96 px tall: the face at the size `large` (72 px), then what a tap does, in the tint, then who sees the photo, in Footnote, muted.
   - **No picture:** the row is the label of the file field. A tap opens the phone's own menu — library, camera or a file — because there is no `capture` here.
   - **A picture:** the row opens a sheet (C11) titled „Foto von {Vorname}": „Neues Foto" (filled, camera icon, full width), the sentence „Löschen geht sofort und lässt sich nicht zurückholen.", „Foto löschen" (plain red) and „Abbrechen". The sheet is the one question before removing, as an iOS action sheet is.
2. For the family, the switch „Im Kurs-Chat zeigen", with its line by age (§8). It shows only when there is a picture and the club's „Kinder im selben Kurs sehen Fotos" is on (§4).
3. For staff, in its place and under the same two conditions, a line saying where the course stands.
4. One note line under the switch.

Nothing on the card is under 44 pt: the row is 96 tall, the switch row 52, the buttons 44.

**States.**

| | What a tap does | The row's small text | Under the row |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Family, no picture | „Foto hinzufügen" | „Freiwillig. Ohne Foto stehen hier deine Anfangsbuchstaben." | — |
| Family, a picture, switch off | „Foto ändern" | „Dein Trainerteam sieht es." | the switch, off · „Die anderen im Kurs sehen nur „{LH}“." |
| Family, a picture, switch on | „Foto ändern" | „Dein Trainerteam und dein Kurs sehen es." | the switch, on · „Die Kinder in deinem Kurs und ihre Familien sehen es im Kurs-Chat. Ausschalten geht jederzeit." |
| Family, the club's setting off | as above | „Dein Trainerteam sieht es." | — (families are not asked) |
| Staff, no picture, the family has a login | „Foto hinzufügen" | „Die Familie bekommt einen Hinweis und kann es ändern." | — |
| Staff, no picture, nobody has signed in for the child yet | „Foto hinzufügen" | „Die Familie sieht es, sobald sie sich angemeldet hat." | — |
| Staff, a picture, the family said yes | „Foto ändern" | „Die Familie sieht es und kann es ändern." | „Im Kurs-Chat sichtbar, weil die Familie zugestimmt hat. Wieder einschalten kann dann nur sie." · the plain tinted button „Im Kurs-Chat ausblenden" |
| Staff, a picture, no yes | „Foto ändern" | as above | „Im Kurs-Chat nicht sichtbar. Das entscheidet die Familie." |
| Staff, a picture, nobody has signed in yet | „Foto ändern" | as above | „Im Kurs-Chat nicht sichtbar. Zustimmen kann die Familie, sobald sie sich angemeldet hat." |

- **Staff never get a switch.** Staff may only take the course view away; a switch that could only go one way would be broken. They get a plain button, and its consequence is said before the tap.
- **{LH} is the child's own initials:** the note shows the family exactly what the others see.

**Without JavaScript.** The label opens the picker, and a visible „Foto speichern" sends the photo; the switch has a „Speichern" button; the sheet opens in place. With JavaScript a chosen photo is drawn smaller and sent at once (§11), a flipped switch takes effect at once, and both buttons are hidden.

**The way back.** A wrong photo: „Neues Foto", or „Foto löschen". A removed photo has deliberately no undo (ADR 0031 §9): the sheet says so before the tap, and a new photo is the way back. The switch turned on by mistake: turn it off; it takes effect at once. Staff hid the photo by mistake: only the family can switch it on again, and the line says so before the tap.

**Banners.** „Foto gespeichert." · „Foto gelöscht." · „Im Kurs-Chat sichtbar." · „Im Kurs-Chat ausgeblendet.", when the family switches it off or staff hide it.

**Refusals**, each one sentence at the top of the same page, the card as it was:

| Case | Sentence |
| --- | --- |
| over the upload limit, or over what the server takes at all (`post_max_size`) | „Die Datei ist zu groß. Höchstens {limit}." |
| more than 24 megapixels | „Dieses Foto hat zu viele Bildpunkte: höchstens 24 Megapixel." |
| more than the memory there is | „Dieses Foto ist zu groß, um es hier zu verkleinern. Bitte ein kleineres wählen." |
| not a JPEG or a PNG the server can read | „Dieses Bild lässt sich nicht lesen. Bitte ein Foto (JPEG oder PNG) wählen." |
| the twenty-first in an hour | „Zu viele Fotos in kurzer Zeit. In einer Stunde geht es wieder." |
| the club's setting off, the family switching on | „Der Verein zeigt im Kurs-Chat keine Fotos." |
| staff switching on | „Einschalten kann nur die Familie, auf der Seite ihres Kindes." |

*Changed 2026-10-09:* the two sizes share one sentence, as every upload's do. Over `post_max_size` PHP keeps no field of the post, so the portal cannot tell that a photo was sent; „Das Foto ist zu groß" for one and „Die Datei ist zu groß" for the other gave the same photo two answers.

**Without gd.** In place of „Foto hinzufügen" or „Foto ändern" the row says „Fotos gehen auf diesem Server noch nicht.", with „Unter „Einstellungen“ → „System“ steht, was fehlt." and the button „Zur Einrichtung" for an administrator, and „Das richtet eine Administratorin ein." for anybody else. A button that could only fail is not offered. A picture already there still shows, and „Foto löschen" still works.

**The bell, when staff add a photo.** The title „Neues Foto von {Vorname}"; the text „{Name} hat es hinzugefügt. Du kannst es jederzeit ändern oder entfernen.", and where it ended a yes, by age (§8): „ Im Kurs-Chat erscheint es erst, wenn ein Elternteil wieder zustimmt." under the age or without a birth date, „ Im Kurs-Chat zeigst du es erst, wenn du wieder zustimmst." from it. It opens the card. A child whose login is a placeholder gets no notice.

## 2. Attendance with faces, and a photo right there

**The rows.** Each row draws the child's face at 40 px. A child without a photo — staff see every photo, so this means none yet — gets the initials with a small camera badge in the tint, and the whole 44 × 44 face is a submit button of the attendance form, labelled „Foto von {Vorname} aufnehmen". A face that has a photo does nothing here: it is changed on the child's page. Without gd there is no camera.

**The face saves the list first.** Marks are saved once, at the end, and a photo is a page load of its own. So the tap first saves the marks made so far and comes back to the same course and day with the photo sheet open: nothing ticked is lost. If nothing was marked, or the day is still to come, nothing is saved and no „0 Einträge" banner appears; the usual banner („2 Einträge gespeichert.") appears only when something was saved.

**Enter still saves.** Enter in a form presses its first submit button, and the faces are submit buttons of this form. So its first button is a copy of „Anwesenheit speichern", drawn but never seen: hidden by its visibility, because a browser passes over a button that is not drawn at all and takes the next one, a face.

**The photo sheet** opens over the list, outside the attendance form, titled „Foto von {Vorname}": the initials at `large`, centred; „Foto aufnehmen", filled and full width with the camera icon, whose file field has `capture="environment"`, so the rear camera opens at once — she is photographing the child in front of her; the note „Die Familie von {Vorname} bekommt einen Hinweis und kann es ändern oder entfernen.", or for a child nobody has signed in for yet „Die Familie sieht es, sobald sie sich angemeldet hat."; and „Abbrechen". The photo goes as soon as it is taken.

**After the photo.** Saved: back to the list for the same course and day, „Foto von {Vorname} gespeichert.", the face in its row. Refused: back with the sheet open and the refusal on top, to try again. An unknown child, a child not in the list, or one who already has a photo: no sheet.

**Taps:** the face, „Foto aufnehmen", then the camera's own two. Without JavaScript, „Foto speichern" is one more.

**„Mehr" beside „Alle: Anwesend"** (the design audit, N3). One row: „Alle: {the first of the club's marks}" — „Alle: Anwesend" — wide on the left, a small plain „Mehr" on the right. „Mehr" opens a sheet with the line „Für die ganze Liste. Gespeichert wird erst mit „Anwesenheit speichern“.", one full-width button for each further mark („Alle: Fehlt", „Alle: Entschuldigt") and „Abbrechen". Choosing one marks every child and shuts the sheet; nothing is saved until „Anwesenheit speichern". A club with a single mark gets no „Mehr"; a fourth mark, such as „Verspätet", lands in the sheet by itself.
- A sheet rather than a fold that opens in place: a fold costs a row or makes „Mehr" jump under her finger, and a whole class „Fehlt" should be a deliberate act.
- Without JavaScript the row stays hidden, as before, and each child is marked one by one.

## 3. Faces elsewhere

Every face goes through `avatar()`: the picture when there is one, its file is there and the one rule (§5) lets whoever is looking see it; the initials otherwise. Every image has `alt=""`, a width and a height, and waits to load until it is scrolled to, except one's own in the top bar. Four sizes: `tiny` 32, `small` 36, the default 40 and `large` 72 px; „Dein Foto" draws its face at 120.

| Where | Size | Staff see | A family sees |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The students list | 40 | every child's face | — |
| The family's overview, their child's card | 40 | — | their child's |
| A course's „Teilnehmer" | 40 | every child's face | — |
| Zugänge, the students' rows | 40 | every child's face | — |
| Zugänge, the team's rows | 40 | the team's faces (§11) | — |
| The chat list, a chat between two | 40 | the child's | the team member's |
| A chat's head | 36 | the child's | the team member's |
| A group's bubbles, at the start of a run | 32 | every child's, and the team's | their own child's, another's by that family's yes, and the team's |
| A group's member sheet, and „Neue Nachricht" | 40 | every face | as in the bubbles |
| The top bar | 32 | one's own | their child's |

A course group's own picture stays its coloured letter. No picture is ever in a mail, a notice in the bell, an invoice, an export, or a page for somebody signed out (ADR 0031 §5).

## 4. The club's settings

On **Einstellungen › Datenschutz**, a card of its own, „Fotos im Kurs", above „Datenschutzerklärung":
- **„Kinder im selben Kurs sehen Fotos"**, a switch, on by default, with the hint „Nur von Kindern, deren Familie zugestimmt hat, und nur im Kurs-Chat. Die Datenschutzerklärung muss es erwähnen." Off, no family is asked and no family sees another's child; the answers given stay, and count again once it is on.
- **„Ab diesem Alter stimmt ein Kind selbst zu"**, under „Erweitert", because it is rarely changed: 13 to 16, 14 by default, with the hint „Darunter, und ohne Geburtsdatum, stimmt ein Elternteil zu. In Österreich 14, in Deutschland 16."

„Größte erlaubte Datei (kB)" names profile photos in its hint.

## 5. What a family that has not said yes sees, and what the others see

- **The family itself** sees its own child's photo everywhere it sees its child: the card, the overview, the top bar and its own row in the group's member sheet; the switch off, with the line saying what the others see. It is never asked again — no card, no notice, no badge (ADR 0031 §8) — and it still sees the faces of the children whose families said yes: seeing the others is never the price of showing one's own.
- **The other families in the course** see that child's initials, in the group's bubbles and member sheet. Initials look exactly like a child with no photo, so nothing shows that a photo exists. They can open no page of that child.
- **Staff** see every photo, always.

**Who sees a child's photo, in plain words:** the club's trainers and administrators; the child's own login; and, with the family's yes, the children in the child's current courses and their families, in the course chat. Nobody else, and never in an e-mail, a notice, an invoice or anything a signed-out person can open.

## 6. The team's pictures

**Mein Konto, for trainers and administrators.** The same card as on the child's page, as the first card, above „Anmeldung": the face at 72 px; „Foto hinzufügen", or „Foto ändern" when there is a picture; and the small text „Das Team und die Familien sehen es." With a picture, the row opens the sheet „Dein Foto": „Neues Foto", „Löschen geht sofort und lässt sich nicht zurückholen.", „Foto löschen" and „Abbrechen". No switch: everybody signed in sees the team's pictures. Banners as on the child's page.

**A family's Mein Konto has no card.** Their picture is the child's, on the child's page.

**Where the team's faces show,** wherever the team showed initials, for families too: the top bar (one's own), the chat list and a chat's head, the trainer's messages in a group, the group's member sheet („Trainerteam") and „Neue Nachricht". A team member without a picture keeps their initials. Zugänge's team rows still draw initials (§11).

## 7. „Dein Foto", once, right after the first password

**Where.** A page of its own, `?page=welcome`. The invitation's activation lands there once, for a family and for a team member, when the server can make a picture; nothing else links to it, so it is never asked again and needs no flag, and opened by hand it is the same harmless step. A login with nothing to put a picture on, or a server without gd, is only shown „Weiter".

**At 320.**
1. The large title „Willkommen, {Vorname}!": a family's by the child's first name, a team member's by the first word of their own.
2. One line of description.
3. The face, 120 px: the initials, or the picture staff already added.
4. The filled „Foto hinzufügen", with the camera icon: the label of the file field, with no `capture`, so the phone offers the camera and the library both. It sends at once.
5. A plain „Überspringen".
6. A footnote saying who sees the picture.

It never offers the course switch, which waits on the child's page (ADR 0031 §8).

| Variant | Line | Buttons | Footnote |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a family | „Möchtest du ein Foto? Es ist freiwillig." | „Foto hinzufügen" · „Überspringen" | „Dein Trainerteam sieht es. Ob auch dein Kurs es sieht, entscheidest du später auf deiner Seite." |
| a family whose trainer already added one | „Dein Trainerteam hat schon ein Foto von dir hinzugefügt." | „Passt so" (filled, a link on) · „Anderes Foto" (tinted) | as above |
| a team member | „Möchtest du ein Foto? Die Familien sehen es in den Chats." | „Foto hinzufügen" · „Überspringen" | „Alle im Portal sehen es. Ändern kannst du es unter „Mein Konto“." |
| a team member who already has one | „Dein Foto ist schon da." | „Passt so" · „Anderes Foto" | as above |

- The second row is where a family first sees a photo the trainer took; „Passt so" is its „Überspringen".
- **After saving or skipping** the step goes on to where the activation landed before: a family to its child's page, a team member to where a sign-in lands (**Übersicht**, or an administrator's start checklist while it is unfinished). After saving, „Foto gespeichert."
- **The activation's banner** says only „Dein Konto ist bereit. Du meldest dich ab jetzt mit {login} an." The welcome is the step's title, and „Noch zu ergänzen" on the child's page says what is missing. The sentence „Willkommen, {name}! Schau kurz, ob alles stimmt, und ergänze, was fehlt. Frag deine Eltern, wenn du etwas nicht weißt." stays only where there is no step: on a server without gd a family's banner goes on with it, so the welcome is said once either way (`first_password_landing()`).
- **Without JavaScript** the label opens the picker, and a visible „Foto speichern" sends it.
- **The way back:** „Überspringen"; and the picture can be changed or deleted later, on the child's page or on Mein Konto.

## 8. The course switch, worded by age (§ 4 Abs. 4 DSG)

**Where.** Inside the switch's own label, as its hint, directly under „Im Kurs-Chat zeigen", so it is read before the tap.

| Case | Hint |
| --- | --- |
| under the age set in „Ab diesem Alter stimmt ein Kind selbst zu" | „Unter {age} schaltet das ein Elternteil ein." |
| no birth date | „Ohne Geburtsdatum schaltet das ein Elternteil ein." |
| from that age | „Das entscheidest du selbst." |

- **A parent's yes stays when the child turns 14.** The switch stays on, the hint becomes „Das entscheidest du selbst.", and nothing asks again.
- The consent record says whose yes it was: `course_sees_picture_by_parent` or `course_sees_picture`.
- The note under the switch is the same in every case, and staff's line stays neutral: „…, weil die Familie zugestimmt hat."
- „A parent switches this on" is said in words, not checked. Whether Art. 8 DSGVO's "reasonable efforts" ask for more is part of the legal reading `ROADMAP.md` keeps for before real families.

## 9. The face beside the greeting (the design audit, N7)

**Where.** On the family's overview only, and only when the login has its child: the child's face at `large`, 72 px, left of „Hallo {Vorname}", leading the title as on a greeting card. On the right it would sit straight under the top bar's face, which is the same picture.

**The tap.** The whole face is a link to the photo row at the top of the child's page (`#picture`), labelled „Dein Profil" for screen readers; the picture keeps `alt=""`. A child without a photo lands where one is added. A login with no child keeps today's empty state and gets no face.

**Wrapping.** The title goes under the face whenever less than 13rem is left beside it — at 320 at the normal text size, and at any width at 150 % or 200 % — so the face never breaks a name inside a word that would not break without it. A hyphenated name breaks at the hyphen, as it already does.

Without JavaScript it is a plain link. No photo, no yes, or a missing file all give the initials, by `avatar()`'s rule.

## 11. Changed while building (2026-10-08)

- **The team has pictures**, after the owner's answers; the first specification was for children only.
- **JPEG and PNG only**, from the security review: a WebP is refused in words, as a GIF is, and the refusal names the two.
- **A chosen photo is drawn smaller in the browser**, 1024 px on its longer side, as a JPEG, before it is sent; the server still holds every limit, as it does for a page without JavaScript. At most 24 megapixels; a photo that would not fit in the memory there is refused in words.
- **A post larger than the server takes** is answered „Die Datei ist zu groß. Höchstens …" on its own page, not „Die Sitzung ist abgelaufen"; since 2026-10-09 every size limit is answered with that one sentence (§1).
- **Staff switching the course view on** is refused in words.
- **Attendance's hidden „Anwesenheit speichern"**, first in the form, so that Enter saves (§2).
- **The bubbles' faces are 32 px** (`tiny`), not 34.
- **Zugänge's team rows** drew initials in the first build, although the second specification listed them among the places with the team's faces. mobile-tester found it, and they show the faces now.
- **From mobile-tester's walk of the first build:** what a tap does on a photo card, „Foto hinzufügen" or „Foto ändern", read „Foto hinzu…" at 320 px at a large text size, and now wraps onto a second line; a face's initials are sized in pixels, as its circle is, so no text size pushes them out of it, as it did „EW" at 200 %; and a photo being sent gave no sign, on a slow network for seconds: now a spinner turns over the face, in place of the initials, and the row does not move.
- **In a course group** the line over a run of bubbles is the writer's name and, for staff, the security review's grey pill, „Trainerin" or „Administrator" (`chat_sender()`); the face beside the run is the writer's.
- **„Dein Foto" has two more cases:** a team member who already has a picture („Dein Foto ist schon da."), and a login with nothing to put a picture on, or a server without gd, which is shown only „Weiter".
- **The activation's banner** is one sentence where „Dein Foto" follows. Without gd there is no step, and a family's banner keeps the welcome, so it is said once either way.
- **gd is optional at setup:** it is shown as „eingeschränkt" and the portal installs; only pictures are missing.
